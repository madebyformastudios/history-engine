# DRAAIBOEK: The ENTIRE History of Iran in 16 Minutes

The scene-by-scene runbook. Single source of truth: every scene, every shot, every cue.

## How to read this runbook

- One scene = one paragraph in `script.txt` (same order). The voiceover uses `script_elevenlabs.txt` (years spelled out, phonetic names).
- Every scene is split into shots (`SHOTS:`), each starting on a cue word. No shot longer than 8 seconds: if a shot runs long after alignment, split it with a second camera move on the same asset.
- `IMG 001` = public/images/001.jpg, `MAP-01` = map below, `PHOTO-01` = public/images/photo-01.jpg (see PHOTOS.md).
- Labels (years, names) use digits. Year plaque on every scene with a date.
- Totals: 63 scenes, 207 shots, 79 illustrations, 29 maps, 14 photos.

## Global style

Same look as the Mongol and Egypt videos: ochre, terracotta, olive, deep blue, parchment; parchment overlay, grain, dust, crossfades of 8 to 12 frames, word-by-word captions.
Map colors: Iran (each dynasty) = terracotta, rivals (Greeks, Rome, Byzantium, Ottomans, Russia) = olive or deep red, other states = muted parchment, foreign zones of influence = hatched.
